Delivery and care

How your tin reaches you cold, where it travels, and what to do when it arrives.

Packed cold

Each order travels chilled, packed to stay between 0 and 4 °C until it reaches your door.

Where we deliver

Milano, the rest of Italy and the European Union. Further afield, ask your host: not every country is possible.

Legal on every tin

Sturgeon caviar travels under CITES rules. Every tin sent outside the EU carries a CITES label with the species, origin and lot.

Packing, courier, delivery times per zone and the full country list come from the client before launch.

Questions

When will my order arrive?

Delivery times for Milano, Italy and the EU are confirmed by the client before launch. You will see the delivery window at checkout.

What should I do when it arrives?

Put the tin straight into the coldest part of the fridge, between 0 and 4 °C. Keep it closed until just before serving. The Caviar Guide has more.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Tick "This is a gift" in your bag and add a note, written by hand on a Dorsiah card. The Gift Box is made for giving.

Can I order 500 g or 1 kg?

Yes, through your host, who also arranges delivery to a villa, yacht or hotel, and company orders with an invoice. Contact your host.

Is it halal or kosher?

Ask your host, who will confirm the certification for your tin before you order.

How can I pay?

Payment methods are confirmed with the client's bank before launch.

Can I return caviar?

Fresh food cannot be returned once delivered, unless it arrives damaged or not cold. If that happens, contact your host the same day.

Final returns policy to be written with the client's lawyer.
