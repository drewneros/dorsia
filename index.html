Ways to serve

  • On its own

    From the back of the hand or a mother-of-pearl spoon, nothing else.

  • Blini

    Small, warm buckwheat blini with a little crème fraîche.

  • Oysters

    A few pearls on a fresh oyster, no lemon needed.

  • Soft eggs

    Scrambled slowly, or a soft-boiled egg with a spoon on top.

  • New potatoes

    Halved, still warm, a spoon of sour cream and caviar.

  • Crudo

    Thin slices of raw fish, olive oil, a few pearls to season.
